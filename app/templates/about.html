Built out of frustration. Designed for clarity.

ArthaLens started as one college student trying to understand where his money was going. It became something bigger.

The Problem

Most Indians have no real visibility into their spending. You get a bank statement — a wall of UPI reference numbers, merchant codes, and cryptic descriptions. You download a finance app — it asks you to link your bank account, charges you a subscription, or simply doesn't understand that "SWIGGY*ORDER" is food, not shopping. The apps that exist are built for someone else. Not for a college student paying rent, splitting bills on UPI, and subscribed to three streaming services they forgot about.

The Story

In December 2025, after trying yet another finance tracker that couldn't tell Swiggy from a shopping expense, Shashwat Narayan — a college student from Hyderabad — decided to build his own. Not as a resume project. As a tool he actually needed. The first version was simple: upload your HDFC statement, get a spending breakdown. But the more he built, the more he realized how deep the problem went. Subscriptions silently draining money every month. Reimbursements never tracked. No way to ask a simple question like "how much did I spend on food last month?" without manually scrolling through hundreds of rows. By June 2026, ArthaLens had become a full financial intelligence system — with smart categorization, anomaly detection, subscription auditing, and an AI assistant that answers questions about your spending in plain English.

Why No Bank Linking?

ArthaLens works differently — you upload your bank statement directly. No third-party bank linking. No sharing your credentials with anyone. Your financial data stays yours.

And we don't keep the statement file itself. The moment it's parsed, the uploaded file is deleted — within seconds. We only retain the extracted transaction details we need to give you insights: the date, merchant, amount, and category. Nothing more. That's it.
